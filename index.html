Building with material, light and time

Quarry designs residential and cultural buildings across the north of England. We work with a small number of clients each year, on projects measured in decades rather than seasons.

Rooted in place

Every project starts on site. We design from what the landscape already gives us.

Honest material

Stone, timber and concrete, left visible. Nothing clad, nothing hidden.

Built to age

Buildings that weather rather than date, and hold their value over decades.

Made to last

Detailing done once, properly. We specify for fifty years, not five.

The Journey of Architecture

Sketches & drawings

Every project starts on paper. We visit the site, take measurements, and work through plans and sections by hand until the proportions feel right. Nothing goes digital until the idea holds up as a drawing.

The drawings become a three-dimensional model. We test light through the seasons, refine the massing, and produce renders so clients can walk through the building long before it exists.
